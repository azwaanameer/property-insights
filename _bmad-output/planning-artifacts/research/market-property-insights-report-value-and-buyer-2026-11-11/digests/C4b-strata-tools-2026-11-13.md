Findings on what Vic buyers get now (Verified for the quoted claims being Cohabit's, via REB 21 Aug 2026): Vic OC certificate has 3 items (latest AGM minutes, building insurance, current fund balances/levies) vs 14 interstate; omits financial history, other lots' arrears, insurance valuation, maintenance plan / engineering reports / defects / lift certs / AESMR, committee minutes, earlier AGMs, SGMs, contract/legal detail. Cohabit says Vic access generally is only after contract signing. Note: this is Cohabit's own marketing analysis.

Gap observation (my inference, not sourced): no tool found that specifically ingests Vic OC certificate + minutes pre-contract and produces a building-level risk report; the AI s32 tools (DueDocs, Unstated) are cheap ($15-29) and generic contract review. Cohabit is the only one with building-level data and a Vic extension, and its AI analysis tool is free, so pricing pressure is downward.
