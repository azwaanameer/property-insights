## Independent criticism (all weak)
- One App Store review of Pricefinder: strata sales appear under a single unit and do not show other lots as properties. Single user, Secondary.
- Consumer complaint sites and an OzBargain thread call CoreLogic estimates "a guessimate tool", some "wildly off by $100k's" (Secondary, anecdotal; the one pissedconsumer figure appears to be a US case).
- Cotality's own AVM consumer-assistance page concedes AVMs assume average condition and cannot adjust for disrepair (Secondary).
- Two Australian agent blogs (lindapowersrealty.pages.dev) claim PropTrack median error 4.8% vs CoreLogic 4.2% on 47 Melbourne properties, and apartments in heavily traded buildings often within 2-3%. Self-published, small sample, domain looks like a template site: treat as Unverified. No independent apartment-specific error rates for any of the three were found.
