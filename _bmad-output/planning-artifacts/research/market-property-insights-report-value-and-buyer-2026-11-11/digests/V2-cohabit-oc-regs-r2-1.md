## C1. "3 items vs 14 interstate; building records mostly accessible only after contract" -- PARTLY (vendor claim confirmed as vendor claim; consistency with Act NOT verified)

- The "3 vs 14" claim originates with Cohabit (a vendor). Cohabit says the Victorian certificate gives 3 items (latest AGM minutes; building insurance details; current fund balances and levy amounts) versus 14 interstate; the other 11 categories "exist but are not required to be shown to buyers before bidding or signing". Source: https://www.realestatebusiness.com.au/industry/32362-victoria-s-strata-disclosure-system-leaves-key-questions-unanswered | Real Estate Business | 20 Aug 2026 (byline 21 Aug) | accessed 2026-11-11 | confidence high that Cohabit said this; marketing register.
- Same claim, near-identical text: https://www.smartpropertyinvestment.com.au/tax-and-legal/28062-the-critical-strata-checks-victorian-buyers-can-t-make | Smart Property Investment | 20 Aug 2026 | confidence high. NOTE: these two are echoes of one Cohabit release = ONE source.
- Pre-contract access: a Cohabit staff member is quoted "In Victoria, access generally isn't available until after a contract is signed." (REB, above). Vendor quote; not independently verified.
- Internal inconsistency in Cohabit's own framing: the same articles say the certificate also shows arrears for the lot, and mentions contracts and legal proceedings (without supporting detail). So "3 items" is a simplification by the vendor, not a count from the Act. 
- Primary text of Owners Corporations Act 2006 (Vic) s151: NOT retrieved (AustLII returned 403). Search snippet only: s151(3) 10 business days to issue; s151 includes statement that further prescribed information can be obtained by inspecting the OC register; CAV sample certificate cites s151 and Owners Corporations Regulations 2018 reg 16 (https://consumer.vic.gov.au/library/Forms/Housing-and-accommodation/Owners-corporations/Sample-owners-corporation-certificate.doc | CAV | undated | snippet only, fetch timed out). Certificate is part of the s32 vendor statement (CAV guide, Apr 2025, snippet). Conclusion: cannot say whether "3" is consistent with the Act; the statutory content list is still to be read.
- Landchecker page (https://landchecker.com.au/products/section-151-certificate-from-owners-corporation | Landchecker | undated) lists a longer content set (fees, unpaid fees, special levies, insurance, funds, liabilities, contracts, unsatisfied notices 12 months, legal proceedings, manager, AGM minutes) but its images refer to NSW, so it is not evidence for Victoria.
