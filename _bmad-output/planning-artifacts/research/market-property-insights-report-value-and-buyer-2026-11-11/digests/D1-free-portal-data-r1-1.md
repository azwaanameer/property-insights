## (d) Contradictions between sources
- Landchecker free scope: product pages say the free report is basics (planning/overlays, no sales history); an Elite Agent article (undated, older) describes broader free PDF access. Resolved in favour of the product page (primary, more specific). Elite Agent not fetched.
- Landchecker pricing: $24.90 current page vs $5.99 in a 2020 press release; the latter is outdated.
- Short-stay: pre-2025 Victorian Supreme Court case law said owners corporations could not ban short stay; 2025 amendments allow it. Resolved by recency.
- REA free vs login: Finder says REA offers "free property reports" but does not say whether sales history is gated; a secondary comparison says an account is needed to track a property. Unresolved.
