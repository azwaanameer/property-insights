## Key takeaways for VicProp
1. Domain gives away a free building page with unit sales, capital growth, rental yield and owner/renter split (Secondary, but strong); sales-comps in a building are table stakes.
2. Neither Domain nor REA nor Allhomes appears to sell a consumer-priced due-diligence report; paid data is B2B (Pricefinder, PropTrack Reports) or Cotality at roughly $180/month.
3. REA's 2026 AI investment is conversational search, valuation Q&A and agent tools; no apartment-building or investor product launched (Verified for NextGen Now).
4. Not found at any portal: strata/body-corporate, building defects, short-stay or Victoria-specific building risk content (absence of evidence only).
