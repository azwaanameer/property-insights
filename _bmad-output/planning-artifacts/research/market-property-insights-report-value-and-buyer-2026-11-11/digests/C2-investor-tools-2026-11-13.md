# C2 - Australian property investor research and suburb-ranking tools

Digest date and access date for every source below: **2026-11-13** (all pages read on that day).

## How to read the evidence labels

- **Verified** = read on the vendor's own (primary) page. Reads were done with a fetch tool that returns a model-written summary of the page, not raw HTML, so figures are as summarised. Re-check any number before putting it in a customer-facing document.
- **Secondary** = reported by a third party or search-result snippet.
- **Unverified** = claim seen but not confirmed on a primary page, or contradicted elsewhere.
- Vendor accuracy claims are always vendor self-reports, even when the page is "Verified". Verified means "the vendor does say this", not "this is true".
- Prices are as shown on 2026-11-13. Most pages carry no "last updated" date; where that is the case it is stated.

## 2. Per-product findings against the seven questions

### Microburbs
1. **Outputs.** Suburb Finder, 4-year suburb growth forecast, street-level medians, AVM, yield, demographics, risk. Data updated: listings and sales daily, suburb metrics weekly, demographics annually (homepage). Verified (vendor).
2. **Prices.** See table. Pricing page shows no date; structured data shows monthly prices; discounts about 20% quarterly, about 30% yearly. Consumer tiers' GST treatment is unstated. The FAQ calls quarterly savings an "annual" discount, which is inconsistent with the agent plans (Verified, flagged by the reader of the page).
3. **Coverage.** National.
4. **Apartments.** See table.
5. **Users, customers, revenue.** None published (homepage shows testimonials and logos only). Verified absence.
6. **Accuracy and criticism.** All self-reported. Figures differ by page: 85% success over 15 years (homepage FAQ); 79% hit rate over 876,000 predictions across 12 years (homepage); on the ML forecast page, walk-forward backtest March 2012 to February 2024, 79.2% of the top 20 picks beat the national average by an average of +6.4% a year, outcomes measured over two years. A search-result snippet quoted a March 2026 whitepaper at "65.4% directional accuracy", but that figure is **not** in the whitepaper page that was read (Unverified). The ML page shows backtests only, no live forward record, and describes outperformance relative to the national average, not absolute growth. The firm also published (28 Feb 2026) a study showing GPT-picked suburbs underperformed (-0.96pp a year across 6,888 predictions per the whitepaper; the companion article says 0.35% a year in one place). That study is houses only and does not disclose that the author sells competing data. No independent audit found.
7. **API and white-label.** API pay-as-you-go from $25 credit, calls from 1c (per-endpoint prices did not render, so per-call prices for forecast or finder endpoints could not be read). MCP server (174 tools, registered in the MCP registry). White-label offered with no price on the page; agents get white-label on yearly plans. Enterprise by contact. Verified.

### SQM Research
1. Vacancy, asking rents, yields, stock on market, days on market; Property Explorer; Boom and Bust forecast report.
2. Property Explorer $128.70 / $344 / $649 / $1,240 (Terms of Service page, undated). Money-back guarantee covers technical delivery only, not forecast accuracy.
3. National. 4. Houses vs units split on vacancy and asking rents (chart-data page). 5. No user counts read. 6. No independent accuracy audit found; a generic forecasting-accuracy search returned nothing about SQM. 7. No API mentioned on pages read; "Custom Data Request" is the bespoke route (price not published).

### Hotspotting
Two agent tiers priced ($299 and $500 a month, 12-month commitment). The $500 tier includes "unlimited white-labelled suburb reports", exportable data and Property Match. Annual and quarterly prices not shown. No user counts. No API mentioned. No independent evidence of forecast accuracy found. The Price Predictor Index is named but not described on pages read. A search for "Holly AI" returned nothing independent, so the chatbot claim rests on the vendor page.

### Suburbtrends
Business-to-business: partner-branded reports, free to verified professionals, 88 SA4 regions, monthly data. No forecasts, no prices, no user counts. White-label is effectively the product (partner branding), price free to partners. Founder is Kent Lardner (APIMagazine profile). A secondary bio mentions co-founding Pricefinder (Unverified, mirror site).

### Propertyology
Commentary plus buyer's agency. No data-tool pricing. Search found no independent audit of its forecasts; one 2022 forecast (Adelaide, Brisbane, Hobart and Canberra detached houses could rise 30-45%) was found but not checked against outcomes (Secondary). Pressley himself criticises population-growth forecasts as a "fool's errand" (Secondary, NestEgg headline).

### DSR Data
One metric (DSR) as a "predictor of capital growth potential". Pricing not published; free quota exhausted on access date. A competitor whitepaper (Microburbs) says a single suburb-level score hides within-suburb variation (Secondary, competitor). No API found.

### InvestorKit
Service business. Its data claims ("nearly $1M a year" on research) are marketing. The free report page gives ten ranked markets with median price, gross yield and growth, but the figures are behind the lead form. The 2018-2025 modelled "up to 3.4 times the national market" is described by the page itself as modelled, not a forecast.

### Property Update
See table; not a tool.

### Newer AI-based entrants (2024-2026)
- **HtAG Eve AI** (free, email-gated) and **HTAG AI Copilot** (press release dated 5 Feb 2026, MCP integration, no price).
- **realestate.com.au NextGen Now** (August 2026, per a trade-site search result; consumer search, not investor ranking) - Secondary.

- Nothing found that is both launched in 2024-2026 and published verifiable accuracy or user numbers.

## 3. Cross-cutting observations (for the product question)

- Suburb-level ranking is crowded, and several vendors give some of it away (Microburbs free finder, SQM free vacancy and yield pages, HtAG Eve free, DSR free lookups when quota allows, Suburbtrends free to professionals).
- Apartment-specific investor tooling is thin. Only SQM (houses vs units series) and Microburbs (unit filter, unit-building supply research) show unit-specific content in what was read. Archistar is for developers.
- No vendor publishes live, forward, audited forecast accuracy. All accuracy figures are in-sample or walk-forward backtests by the vendor.
- Price points for paid access cluster at roughly $77-$135 a month (Microburbs consumer), about $129 a month (SQM), $180-$320 a month (Cotality), $299-$500 a month (Hotspotting agent tiers).
- API and white-label: only Microburbs publishes an API price structure (and white-label without price). Hotspotting and Suburbtrends offer white-label reports to professionals.
