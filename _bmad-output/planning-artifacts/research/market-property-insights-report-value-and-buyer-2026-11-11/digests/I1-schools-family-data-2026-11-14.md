Verdict key: Verified = I opened the publisher page and read the licence/terms. Secondary = only seen via a search snippet, mirror or third party. Unverified = could not open, or terms unclear.

Not legal advice. "Commercial-use verdict" answers: can a small company put this data, or figures derived from it, in a paid report/web product?

### What is NOT available for Victoria (confirmed or not found)
- A school-zone API: only a yearly ZIP.
- Zones for non-government, selective, specialist, English-language or community schools (excluded by the dataset itself).
- Enrolment in the School Locations CSV (use the FTE enrolments file or My School).
- Open school-quality scores. ICSEA/NAPLAN are bound by My School terms (no commercial use without written permission). VCE results terms could not be read. Dept performance summaries are staff-only.
- Open childcare/kinder quality ratings with a clear commercial licence.
- Whether the School Locations CSV carries latitude/longitude: not stated on the page; check the file. If not, schools must be geocoded (G-NAF or similar) to compute distance.

### Design implications
- Zone lookup is the only strong, clean, commercial-safe layer: CC BY 4.0, but carry the "do not rely on for purchase" caveat and the data year (2026 vs 2027).
- Distance to schools and school type/sector/enrolment are buildable from CC BY files (locations CSV + FTE enrolments + zones) with no quality score.
- Any quality figure (ICSEA, NAPLAN, VCE, NQS) needs written permission from ACARA, VCAA or ACECQA before it goes in a paid product. Ask first.

## 3. Blocked, failed or unclear (do not treat as checked)
- ACECQA pages (terms-of-use, copyright, registers, snapshots, NQA IT terms): HTTP 403 on every attempt. Terms content is from a search snippet only. I did not try to bypass this.
- VCAA copyright and statistics pages: 404 on the URLs I tried.
- ACARA Data Access Protocols: only the superseded 2012 PDF opened and it was unreadable; 2015 revision not opened. ICSEA availability and file format come from third-party descriptions.
- data.gov.au QLD mirror and ACT ArcGIS page: 403 or empty.
- Full data.vic.gov.au disclaimer page not opened.
- File contents (field names, internal formats, coordinates) of every ZIP/CSV were not downloaded or inspected.
- Search snippets about TAS, WA recency, ACT licence are secondary and flagged above.
