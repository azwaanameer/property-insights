## Key findings
1. Landchecker's free report already covers zones, overlays, bushfire and heritage sensitivity for every Australian address; its Premium report ($24.90 one-off) adds flood/landslide, easements, permits and sales history. A zoning/overlay-only product has no price room against this.
2. Neither Landchecker's property report nor Cotality's report page mentions apartment, building, strata or OC content. Landchecker sells OC documents only as separate Vic add-ons (Basic Report, s151 certificate) with no analysis layer; title documents are excluded from plans. Building-level content appears to be a gap in these tools (Landchecker report: Verified; Cotality: Verified for that page only).
3. Landchecker's published scale numbers are data volume (23,800+ addresses and 20,300+ permits/DAs added monthly; 7.3M+ planning data points); no user or customer count published. Its target is professionals (agents, developers, valuers, lawyers); consumers get the free tier.
4. Independent criticism is essentially absent: Landchecker 0 Trustpilot reviews, no G2/SourceForge reviews; Archistar 2 Capterra reviews, 4.0 overall, support and value 2.0 each (one review each). No accuracy complaints found, which reflects lack of reviews rather than evidence of quality.
5. Victorian disclosure context (Secondary): a RE Business piece says Vic strata disclosure gives 3 information items vs 14 in an interstate equivalent, and OC information often arrives after contract signing. This supports a building/OC gap for Vic buyers.
