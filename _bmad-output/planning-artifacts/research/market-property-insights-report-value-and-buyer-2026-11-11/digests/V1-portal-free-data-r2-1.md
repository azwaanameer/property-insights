# V1 r2-1: What REA and Domain show free (verification, attempt to disconfirm the gap)

Accessed 2026-11-11 for all items. All direct fetches of domain.com.au and help.domain.com.au returned HTTP 403; web.archive.org is blocked by the fetch tool. Items marked SNIPPET rest on a search-engine result summary (an AI-generated digest of the results page), not on a page I read. Those are the weakest items.

## Headline (read this first)

1. Domain HAS a building-level page ("building-profile") for apartment buildings. This contradicts any assumption that no portal does building-level data. Evidence is snippet-only (see A3).
2. The ANZ free report is confirmed as free, no login, emailed. Its comparable sales are area-based (not a same-building filter), but some same-building units appear incidentally.
3. Nothing I could retrieve shows owners corporation fees, building age/defects or short-stay rules on either portal.
4. REA (realestate.com.au) logged-out property and suburb page content remains NOT directly evidenced.

## A4. ANZ free emailed property report (read directly; strongest evidence)

- https://www.anz.com.au/personal/home-loans/getting-started/property-profile-reports/ | ANZ | no pub date on page | confidence high | fetched page, not a snippet (read via fetch summarizer, so one step removed from raw HTML).
  - Page calls them "free comprehensive property reports". Sales Report (16 pages): sales and rental history of the property (price, sale type, selling agent where available), similar recently sold nearby properties, a PropTrack price range estimate for "listed and unlisted properties", suburb insights incl. demographics/amenities. A 19-page Equity version adds estimated equity.
  - No login mentioned; you submit property details on a form (propertyprofilereport.anz.com.au) and the report is emailed. Estimate "NOT a valuation"; may be missing if few comparables; no restriction by property type stated.
- Sample report PDF: https://exclusives.anz.com.au/content/dam/anzcomau/documents/pdf/anz-sample-property-profile-report.pdf | ANZ/PropTrack | sample dated 2 June 2022 (OLD, over 6 months; template may have changed) | confidence high for 2022 layout | fetched and read in full.
  - Sample was a UNIT (1/5 Paget St, Hughesdale; Lot/Plan shown). Contents: photos; price range ($785k-$1,065k, midpoint $920k); points of interest; median days on market for similar properties; 7 "recently sold units" (price, agency, sold date, sale type, listed date, beds/baths/cars); 7 units for sale (advertised price); 7 units for rent (weekly rent, features); suburb profile with 5-year house/unit medians, annual change, 12-month median, rental yield, auction clearance (houses and units, vs state and national), highest/lowest sold, discounting, dwellings count; ABS demographics (age, own/rent split); sales by bedroom count and price band; agency rankings (days on market, auction clearance).
  - Sample "sales history" section for the subject property itself was described on the contents page ("features, estimated price range and sales history") but I did not see a visible history table in the sample.
  - Comparables are "chosen based on property features, recency of sale and distance". There is NO same-building filter. Incidentally, the sample's lists included other units at 568 Neerim Rd (Unit 10 sold, price "Not Available"; Units 18 and 22 for sale; Unit 7 for rent) and 50 Poath Rd (Units 9 and 16 for rent). So same-building comps can appear by proximity, but are not guaranteed or labelled.
  - Several sold prices showed "Not Available"; no owners corporation fees, building age, or strata data in the sample.

## Evidence against the gap claim (A5)

The idea "no free source shows same-building unit sales" is weakened, not disproved:

1. Domain building-profile pages (snippet-only) appear to list recent sales for units in the building and per-unit buy/resale pairs, plus occupancy mix and average tenure. Per-unit resale pairs are same-building sale history. This is the strongest counter-evidence; it needs a direct, logged-out check of one page.
2. Domain "sold listings" pages by suburb and property type reportedly show unit sales within one building, with some prices withheld (https://www.domain.com.au/sold-listings/melbourne-vic-3004/apartment-unit-flat/ | SNIPPET-ONLY, a search digest).
3. ANZ's free report (confirmed) surfaces nearby unit comparables by proximity and features; in the sample, several are in the subject's own or neighbouring buildings (A4).
4. Landgate (WA) says its single-address report shows what has sold in a street or strata (https://www.landgate.wa.gov.au/property-reports/single-address-report | SNIPPET-ONLY; paid status not confirmed; WA only, irrelevant to Melbourne).

What the gap likely still is (not proven): I found no evidence on either portal of owners corporation fees, building age/defect history, number of short-stay units, cladding, sinking fund, or a building-level price-per-sqm or like-for-like comparison. Data quality is also variable (null prices, outlier errors seen in Domain building history). Reasonable framing: building-level sold lists exist free on Domain; interpretation, quality-checking and strata/risk context may not.
