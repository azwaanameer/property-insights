# C5a Demand evidence digest (FINAL) - 2026-11-13

Budget used: 11 searches, 8 fetches. Status key: Verified = primary page read via WebFetch; Secondary = search-result summary or third-party page; Unverified = aggregator estimate or not confirmed.
All access dates 2026-11-13.

## Headline: the evidence is thin. No survey was found that measures what investors spend on research data. Demand evidence is indirect (vendor price lists, funding, buyer's-agent tooling).

## (a) Buyer's agent fees
- Percentage: REBAA benchmark 2-3% + GST + engagement fee; other guides 1.5-3%; Brisbane 2-2.75% + GST. Secondary (search summary of agent/lead-gen sites). https://propertyupdate.com.au/how-much-do-buyers-agents-charge-in-australia/
- Fixed fee: ~$10k-18k for capital-city houses near median; wider $6k-30k by price bracket. Secondary. https://www.yourpropertyguide.com.au/guides/buyers-agent-cost-australia
- Melbourne full service typical $6,500-31,500 (Sydney $7,000-36,000). Secondary. https://whatsthedamage.com.au/buyers-agent-cost-melbourne/
- Partial service: auction/negotiation-only $600-1,500 per property (one source) to $1,500-3,500 per auction (another). Secondary.
- Caveats: fees unregulated; sources are mostly commercial. Cost likely capitalised into cost base for investors (one source; confirm with accountant).
- Implication: a $29-$99 report is 0.1-1% of a typical buyer's-agent fee; the nearest paid substitute is the $600-3,500 single-auction service.

## (b) Investor research behaviour and willingness to pay
- PIPA 2026 Investor Sentiment Survey: 626 investors, Aug 2026. Verified (page read) that it contains no data on research tools, spend or buyer's-agent use. https://pipa.asn.au/advocacy/investor-sentiment-survey/ . 96.1% say advice providers should have formal training; 66% say QPIA qualification would influence adviser choice (Verified, same page; relates to advice, not data).
- PIPA 2025 survey: 854 respondents (Secondary). A "share who work with a property professional, up from 75%" was seen in a search summary but not confirmed; Unverified. The PDF links fetched returned only a navigation page. https://www.pipa.asn.au/media/ad0hseqc/pipa_investor-survey-report-2025_v03.pdf
- API Property Sentiment Report Q2 2026 (18th edition) exists; sentiment focus. Not read. https://www.apimagazine.com.au/docs/investor-report/API_Property-Sentiment-Report_Q2-2026.pdf
- No 2023-2026 survey on investor tool usage or spend found. GAP.
- Price anchors for paid products:
  - Cotality (CoreLogic/RP Data) for investors: plan at $229/mo, min $2,748 incl GST over 12 months. Secondary (vendor page via search). https://www.cotality.com/au/products/rp-data-for-investors
  - CoreLogic one-off Property Report listed at $43. Secondary. https://corelogic.com.au/reports/property-profile-report
  - Hotspotting: Premium Plus $299/mo (buyer's agents) and Property Pro $500/mo (buyer's agents working with clients, "Most Popular"), both monthly billing with 12-month commitment; 7-day free trial; no subscriber count stated. Verified. https://www.hotspotting.com.au/memberships . Note a search summary said $250/mo, so price has moved or tiers differ; use the page figure.
  - Microburbs: pay-as-you-go credit from $25, per-call from 1c, annual team/white-label plans (12 months for price of 10). Secondary. https://www.microburbs.com.au/subscription-options
  - HTAG investor subscription (AI credits, heatmaps, branded suburb PDFs). Secondary. https://www.htag.com.au/register-pro/
- Pattern: the premium tiers are priced for buyer's agents (white-label, unlimited reports) rather than individual buyers. Hotspotting's most popular tier is the buyer's-agent tier at $500/mo.

## (c) Proptech data company figures
- Landchecker (Verified from company page): Tanarra Capital and syndicate acquired a controlling interest; PEXA exited; RACV remains minority; 2,800 organisations, 8,000 paying end users; revenue grew 70% in last FY; price and date not stated. https://landchecker.com.au/articles/landchecker-acquisition-announcement/ . Earlier rounds (Secondary): 2018 $1.85M (RACV $750k for 13.5%), 2019 $3.5M led by Ruslan Kogan; CB Insights total $3.87M. https://landchecker.com.au/articles/kogan-leads-funding-round-for-landchecker/ ; https://www.cbinsights.com/company/landchecker . Revenue estimate $2.57M is a third-party aggregator (Unverified). Landchecker's customers are mostly B2B (organisations).
- Cohabit (Verified from article): $1.2M seed, 10 July 2025, investors John Mullen, Shant Kradjian, Christopher Kerin, Michael Cindric; ~50,000 building profiles six months after soft launch; 15 strata inspection firms on its Expert Portal; pricing, revenue and who pays not disclosed. https://www.startupdaily.net/topic/residential-building-proptech-cohabit-moves-in-on-1-2-million-raise/ . A CB Insights/Crunchbase "$4.33M 2019" entry looks like a data error (Unverified).
- Microburbs: no funding rounds found. ZoomInfo estimates revenue $6.4M, 1-10 staff (Unverified aggregator estimate). https://www.zoominfo.com/c/microburbs/409924382
- Hotspotting: no subscriber/revenue/funding figures found. Suburbtrends: no figures found (consulting/data firm; Kent Lardner). Neighbourlytics (lifestyle data) was acquired by REA Group (Nov 2025 per Startup Daily tag page; Secondary), showing portal acquirers buy this layer. https://www.startupdaily.net/tag/proptech/
- Read: small but real market, mostly B2B or buyer's-agent/professional-led. Funding sizes are $1-4M; no evidence of a large consumer revenue pool.

## (d) Free portal data displacing paid reports
- Direct evidence of displacement: none found. GAP.
- Free supply is real: Finder guide says Domain, realestate.com.au and ANZ offer detailed free property reports with sales history and comparables. Secondary. https://www.finder.com.au/how-to-find-out-property-past-sales-history
- Banks and credit unions give away CoreLogic/RP Data property reports and PropTrack-sourced reports (ANZ); free versions lack a platform login. Secondary. https://www.anz.com.au/personal/home-loans/calculators-tools/property-profile-reports/ ; https://www.huntergalloway.com.au/rp-data-free
- Domain building-profile pages exist with unit counts, owner-occupier vs rented split, recent sales (example pages seen in search; free access not confirmed). Secondary. https://domain.com.au/building-profile/81-queens-road-melbourne-vic-3004
- Implication: comps and sales history are commodity; paid value must come from interpretation, forward-looking or non-portal layers (strata, risk, building-level).

## (e) Complaints about paying for reports
- ProductReview.com.au: many one-star RP Data reviews calling it a waste of money, data irrelevant/outdated/wrong, hard to use, free public sources give the same data. Secondary; anecdotal. https://www.productreview.com.au/listings/rp-data
- Automated reports can differ 10-20%+ from true value where sales are few (LocalAgentFinder; commercial interest). Secondary. https://www.localagentfinder.com.au/blog/property-report-complete-guide
- Propertybuyer (a buyer's agency, Rich Harvey, 23 Jul 2025) argues data overload causes "analysis paralysis", suburb medians mislead, and buyers' agents add value by selecting/interpreting data and accessing off-market sales. Verified (page read) but self-interested, and the title ("free property reports can cost you thousands") does not match the body. https://www.propertybuyer.com.au/blog/how-free-property-reports-can-cost-you-thousands
- No Reddit complaints retrieved (only thread listings). Old PropertyChat threads (2017-2019) say free info suffices. Secondary. https://www.propertychat.com.au/community/threads/rp-data-core-logic.32309/
