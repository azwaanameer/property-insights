Investor snapshot

Income is solid and the entry price is falling, but this market has not grown in ten years. The case rests on yield and a good purchase price, not on capital growth.

  • YieldAbout 6.9% gross at the middle sale price; 7.9% if the latest Domain sale ($445k) is the right comparison.
  • GrowthDocklands unit prices moved −0.6% a year over five years and −1.0% over ten. This building's recent sales show −2.55% a year over ten years.
  • Competition7 of 21 homes for sale here are the same layout, and asking prices start at $370,000.
  • Short stayEstimated $60,836 a year, about $25,736 more than the lease costs, before running costs. Rules in the building are unknown.

Renter snapshot

At $675 a week this is cheaper than most similar apartments in the building, and it has been advertised for 65 days, which gives you room to negotiate.

  • Price7% below the building median ($725) and 16% below the official Docklands median for new 2-bed leases ($800). Second-lowest of 12 similar units.
  • TrendDocklands rents rose +6.7% in the past year, so expect a rise at renewal.
  • UpfrontBond $2,933. Furnishing is disputed: the listing says unfurnished.
  • UnknownLease length, building noise, how many neighbours run short stays (53 Airbnb listings operate here), and building condition.

Buyer snapshot (to live in)

You would be buying below what the same layout cost in this building a few months ago, and below where it sold ten years ago, with plenty of choice.

  • PriceSimilar units sold for $445k to $532k in the past six months. 7 are for sale now, from $370,000.
  • Ten yearsThe latest sale in the building (unit 413) was $445k, against $582,000 paid in June 2016.
  • Who lives hereAbout 55% of homes are rented, an estimate from Domain. Expect fewer owner-occupiers and more turnover.
  • UnknownOwners corporation fees, special levies, defects and the building's rules. These decide whether it is a comfortable place to own.

What should you ask next?

Short answer. A short list for the agent, written for your situation.

  1. Short stays: Does the owners corporation restrict short-term stays? Please send the current rules.
  2. Costs: What are the annual owners corporation fees, and are any special levies or major works planned?
  3. Price: A unit of this layout sold for $445k on 9 Nov 2026. What has the owner of unit 310 been offered?
  4. Tenancy: If rented, what is the lease end date and the current rent?
  5. Documents: Can you share the owners corporation certificate and the last two years of meeting minutes?

  4. Costs: Which utilities and building services are included in the rent?
