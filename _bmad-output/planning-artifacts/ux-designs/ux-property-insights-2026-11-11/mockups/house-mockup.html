What is Cranbourne North known for?

Free

Short answer. A fast-growing family suburb about 44 km south-east of the CBD, built since the early 2000s on former market gardens. It is large, mostly detached houses, and has one of the most culturally mixed populations in outer Melbourne.

  1. Before the 2000sMarket gardens and farmland north of Thompsons Road. The suburb takes its name from that position.
  2. 1994A post office opens at Cranbourne North, on 21 March.
  3. 2003Construction begins on the Camden Green estate, one of the largest developments in the suburb.
  4. 202124,683 people live in Cranbourne North on Census night, and 95% of homes are detached houses.
