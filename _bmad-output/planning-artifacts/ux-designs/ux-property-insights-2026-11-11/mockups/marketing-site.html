    What it is

    One address in. One report out.

    Buying property means pulling facts from a dozen places: the title, the council, the census, the rental data, the building. VicProp does that for one address and writes the answers in plain English, with the source and date beside every figure.

    Features

    Built to be checked, not just read.

    Every figure has a label

    Observed means it came from a dataset. Estimated means we worked it out. Reported means a public property page said so and we did not check the title. Unknown means we could not find out.

    Land measured, not guessed

    Block size comes from the parcel outline. For 14 Lucinda Lane we measured about 447 m² and listings report 448 m².

    Down to the building

    The national address file lists every flat. Docklands East Tower has 733, so you can see how big the building is before you buy one.

    Short-stay only when it pays

    The Airbnb section appears only when estimated income beats a normal lease by at least 40%. Otherwise the report says why it is left out.

    A rating that states its limits

    The conditions rating is tested on Victorian unit suburbs: a top-fifth suburb grew faster than a bottom-fifth one 74% to 80% of the time. It is not a price forecast, and it is not shown where it was not tested.

    Gaps stay visible

    If a source is missing for your address the report says what is missing and what to ask. It never fills a hole with a guess.

    Planning in plain English

    Zones and overlays are translated into what they mean for you, with the gazette date beside each one.

    A return calculator you control

    Set price, rent, costs and growth yourself. It is labelled an illustration, never a forecast.

    An A4 PDF

    Every section open, sources listed, ready to share with a partner, lender or client.

    Questions

    Before you try it.

    What do I get without paying?

    The free sections: what the property is, rent, price, schools, transport, what is nearby, risks, planning and the questions to ask. Locked sections show what they cover and stay blurred until you unlock them.

    Which addresses work?

    Victorian addresses. Other states are not covered yet. This mockup has full data for two addresses and shows how the rest would look.

    Is the conditions rating a price forecast?

    No. It describes a suburb on three Census markers and was tested on past Victorian unit prices. We do not predict prices.

    Where do the sale prices of one house come from?

    Suburb medians come from the Valuer-General. Sales of an individual house come from public property pages found for that address. They are marked Reported, with the link and date, and are not checked against the title register.

    Why is there no Airbnb section in my report?

    We show it only when estimated short-stay income is at least 40% above the long-term rent for the same home. Most houses do not clear that, so the report leaves it out and says so.

    What if the report does not load after I pay?

    You are refunded. This wording is a proposal for launch.

    Is this financial advice?

    No. It is general information from public data. Talk to a licensed adviser, a conveyancer and a building inspector before you buy.
