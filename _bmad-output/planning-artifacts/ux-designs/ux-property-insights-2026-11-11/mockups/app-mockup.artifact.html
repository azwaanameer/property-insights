What is Docklands known for?

Free

Short answer. A waterfront precinct on the western edge of the CBD, rebuilt over the last three decades from Melbourne's old port into apartments, offices and an events stadium.

  1. 1887 to 1892Victoria Dock is dug out of low wetlands, a 37.6-hectare port.
  2. 1980sShipping moves downstream. The old docks near the city become available for redevelopment.
  3. 1991The Docklands Authority is formed to redevelop the area.
  4. 2020East Tower at Melbourne Quarter completes: 40 levels, designed by Fender Katsalidis for Lendlease and Mitsubishi Estate Asia.
  5. 202115,495 people live in Docklands on Census night.
