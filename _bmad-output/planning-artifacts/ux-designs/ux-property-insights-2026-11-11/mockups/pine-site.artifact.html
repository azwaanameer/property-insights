    Questions

    Before you rely on it.

    Which addresses are covered?

    Victoria only for now: houses, townhouses and flats. Other states are not covered yet.

    What do I get before I pay?

    A free preview of every address. It covers the property basics, block size, rent and price, school zones, transport, what is nearby, and the planning, flood, bushfire and heritage flags. No account is needed. Sections marked Full report unlock with payment.

    Is the conditions rating a price forecast?

    No. It ranks Victorian apartment suburbs on three Census markers. Across past test windows, top-fifth suburbs out-grew bottom-fifth ones 74 to 80% of the time, so roughly one in four did not. It is not shown for houses or other states.

    Why is there no short-stay section in my report?

    It appears only when our estimate is at least 40% above the long-term rent for the same home. Most houses do not clear that, so the report leaves it out and says so. We do not check whether short stays are allowed in the building. Ask the owners corporation.

    Does it replace an inspection, strata documents or a buyer’s agent?

    No. It is general information, not financial, legal, planning or valuation advice. Rooms, floor area and past sales of a single home come from public listings, are marked Reported, and are not checked against the title. Use the report to decide what to ask your agent, conveyancer, inspector and owners corporation.

    What if the report does not load after I pay?

    We refund you in full. This does not limit your rights under the Australian Consumer Law.
